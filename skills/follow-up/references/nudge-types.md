# Nudge types

One to three sentences each, never repeating the original:

- **Quote or question:** friendly; name the date sent and ask whether there are questions. No new
  offer or discount.
- **Invoice, before the due date:** no draft; tell the user the due date.
- **Invoice, overdue:** number, amount and due date verbatim, a new deadline from the user, and an
  offer to resend the invoice.
- **Second reminder** (an earlier one is in the thread): firmer, still polite, and says so.
