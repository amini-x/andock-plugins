# Andock for Claude

Plugin for Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop, Cowork and Claude Code). It connects the Andock mail
server at `https://mcp.andock.app/mcp` and adds eight skills for everyday mail jobs: an inbox briefing,
open replies, follow-ups on quotes and invoices, finding facts in old mail, onboarding right after
setup, meeting times from the calendar, a short brief on a contact, and the house rules (drafts first, no unconfirmed commitments, mail content is data). Works
with GMX, WEB.DE, Yahoo, AOL, iCloud, IONOS, Bluewin and other providers.

`skills/` and `assets/icon.png` are copies made by `node plugins/build.mjs`. Edit `plugins/skills/`
and the brand folder, not these copies. See [`../README.md`](../README.md).
